About Us

Research That Ships

SAEDGE AI Automations Private Limited is revolutionizing quality control in Indian manufacturing through innovative, edge-native AI visual inspection systems — anchored at IIT Palakkad.

One proprietary Edge AI vision engine powers every product we ship. It offers greater accuracy, lower latency, and cross-industry adaptability — making advanced visual inspection accessible to manufacturers of every size, especially India's MSMEs.

Our Mission

Democratizing advanced manufacturing quality control through innovative, cost-effective AI solutions that empower businesses of all sizes to achieve excellence in product quality.

Our Vision

Enabling every manufacturer, regardless of size, to achieve world-class quality standards through intelligent, affordable visual inspection technology that drives operational excellence.

Learning Kit

SAEDGE AI Learning Kit

The hands-on AI learning kit where students drag, connect and run ideas on real ESP32 hardware — no syntax required to start.

Learn by building, not memorising

A visual workflow editor, real ESP32 hardware, and on-device AI vision — in one classroom-ready kit.

  • Drag-and-connect block editor — zero syntax to begin
  • Runs on real ESP32 hardware: sensors, LEDs, buzzer, servo and motor
  • On-device AI vision — face, emotion, object, colour, motion, shape, gesture and QR detection
  • Offline-first: AI runs locally on the classroom computer, not the cloud
  • Built-in curriculum, lab manual and CBSE AI-aligned project paths
  • Bilingual interface (English and Hindi); projects save and reopen

Visual Workflow Editor

Select blocks, connect ports, configure and run — then tweak and rerun.

Computer Vision Lab

Eight ways for machines to see, processed locally frame by frame.
